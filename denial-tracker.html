How to use

  • Fill in one row per denial letter you receive.
  • Note the appeal deadline (usually 30–180 days from the denial date, depending on your plan and state).
  • Mark each step as you complete it: letter received, appeal submitted, response received, resolved.
  • Keep copies of every letter, EOB, and your appeal in a folder (physical or digital).

Common denial reasons to look for

  • Service not covered / not a covered benefit
  • Not medically necessary
  • Prior authorization not obtained
  • Out-of-network provider
  • Missing or incorrect coding
  • Duplicate claim
  • Timely filing limit exceeded
  • Benefit limit reached

Tips

  • Call the number on the back of your insurance card and ask for the appeals department.
  • Ask for a copy of your full claim file and the plan's medical policy.
  • Keep a phone log: date, time, who you spoke with, and what they said.
  • Send appeals by certified mail with return receipt requested, or through your insurer's secure member portal with a confirmation.
  • If your internal appeal is denied, you may have the right to an external review.
