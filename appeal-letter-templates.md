# Appeal Letter Templates

Plain-English appeal letters you can fill in, print, and send to your insurer. Education only — not legal advice.

Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

## Before you send

- Send your appeal by certified mail with return receipt requested, or upload it through your insurer's secure member portal and save the confirmation.
- Keep copies of everything: your letter, the denial letter, EOBs, medical records, and any letters from your provider.
- Note the appeal deadline on your denial letter. Don't miss it.
- Ask your provider's office for a letter of medical necessity supporting your claim.

## Template 1: Standard appeal (internal appeal)

Use this for most first-level appeals after a denial.

---

[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Phone number]
[Email address]
[Date]

[Insurance Company Name]
[Appeals Department / Address from denial letter]
[City, State ZIP]

Re: Appeal of Claim Denial
Member ID: [Your Member ID]
Claim number: [Claim number from denial/EOB]
Date of service: [Date of service]
Provider: [Provider name]
Billed amount: [Amount]

To the Appeals Department:

I am writing to formally appeal the denial of claim [claim number] for [service/procedure] performed on [date of service] by [provider name]. The denial was communicated in a letter dated [date of denial letter], with reason: [reason for denial from denial letter].

I believe this denial was made in error because:

[Explain in your own words why the service should be covered. For example: my doctor determined this service was medically necessary, the service is a covered benefit under my plan, or prior authorization was obtained.]

Enclosed with this letter:
- [ ] Copy of the denial letter
- [ ] Copy of the Explanation of Benefits (EOB)
- [ ] Letter of medical necessity from my provider
- [ ] Relevant medical records or notes
- [ ] Other: [describe]

I request that you review my claim again and overturn the denial. Please respond in writing within [your plan's appeal response timeframe, e.g., 30 days] at the address above.

Sincerely,

[Your signature]
[Your printed name]

Enclosures: [list what you're including]

cc: [Provider name, if applicable]

---

## Template 2: Appeal with provider support

Use this when your doctor or provider has written a letter of medical necessity and you're submitting it with your appeal.

---

[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Phone number]
[Email address]
[Date]

[Insurance Company Name]
[Appeals Department]
[City, State ZIP]

Re: Appeal of Claim Denial with Supporting Documentation
Member ID: [Your Member ID]
Claim number: [Claim number]
Date of service: [Date of service]
Provider: [Provider name]

To the Appeals Department:

I am appealing the denial of claim [claim number] for [service/procedure] on [date of service], denied on [date of denial] for the reason: [reason].

My provider, [provider name / credentials], has determined this service is medically necessary for my condition. Their supporting letter and relevant records are enclosed.

I ask that you reconsider this claim in light of the enclosed documentation and approve coverage under my plan benefits.

Enclosed:
- [ ] Provider letter of medical necessity
- [ ] Medical records / office notes
- [ ] Copy of denial letter and EOB
- [ ] Other: [describe]

Please send your written decision to the address above within [timeframe].

Sincerely,

[Your signature]
[Your printed name]

---

## Template 3: External review request

Use this after your internal appeal has been denied and you want an independent third party to review your case. Your denial letter should explain whether you're eligible and how to request it.

---

[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Phone number]
[Email address]
[Date]

[Insurance Company Name or External Review Organization]
[Address from denial letter]
[City, State ZIP]

Re: Request for External Review
Member ID: [Your Member ID]
Claim number: [Claim number]
Date of internal appeal denial: [Date]

To Whom It May Concern:

My internal appeal for claim [claim number] ([service/procedure], [date of service]) was denied on [date]. I am requesting an external review of that decision by an independent reviewer, as permitted under my plan and applicable law.

Enclosed:
- [ ] Copy of the internal appeal denial letter
- [ ] Copy of my original appeal and supporting documents
- [ ] Any additional records or letters

Please contact me at [phone] or [email] with next steps.

Sincerely,

[Your signature]
[Your printed name]

---

## What to include in any appeal

- Your full name, member ID, and contact information
- The claim number and date of service
- The specific reason for denial (from the denial letter)
- A clear, simple statement of why you disagree
- Supporting documents (EOB, denial letter, provider letter, medical records)
- What you're asking the insurer to do (approve the claim, overturn the denial)
- A request for a written response within a specific timeframe
