Payment-Plan Negotiation Script

Before you call

  • Have the bill and EOB in front of you.
  • Know the total amount owed and the due date.
  • Decide what monthly payment you can realistically afford.
  • Ask if the provider offers financial assistance / charity care (nonprofit hospitals often do).
  • Get any agreement in writing before you pay.

Call script

You: "Hi, my name is [your name], and I'm calling about a medical bill, account number [account number]. I received a bill for [amount] dated [date]. I'd like to talk about setting up a payment plan."

Billing office: [They may offer a plan or ask what you can pay.]

You: "Before we set anything up, can you confirm this bill matches my insurance Explanation of Benefits? My EOB shows I owe [amount]. I want to make sure the charges are correct first."

You: [If there's a discrepancy] "The EOB shows [amount], but this bill says [amount]. Can you review and correct it before we discuss payment?"

You: [If the bill is correct] "I can't pay [full amount] right now. I can afford to pay [$X per month]. Can we set up a payment plan for that amount?"

You: [If they say no or counteroffer] "I understand. Can you offer any discounts for paying in full, or a financial assistance / charity care program? I'd also like to know if there's a no-interest payment plan available."

You: [If they agree] "Great. Can you send me the payment plan agreement in writing? I'd like to confirm: the monthly amount, the due date each month, how to pay, whether there's any interest or fees, and what happens if I miss a payment."

After the call

  • Get the agreement in writing (email or mail).
  • Confirm the first payment date and amount.
  • Set a reminder so you don't miss a payment.
  • Keep records of every payment you make.
  • If your situation changes, call before you miss a payment.

Questions to ask the provider

  • Is there a discount for paying the bill in full today?
  • Do you offer a no-interest payment plan?
  • What's the minimum monthly payment?
  • Is there a fee to set up the plan?
  • Do you offer financial assistance or charity care? How do I apply?
  • Will this bill go to collections if I set up a plan and make payments on time?
  • Can you send the agreement in writing?
